### Landscape Observations

*   **From SFT to RLVR (Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards):** Pre-2025 TS-captioning largely relied on supervised next-token prediction, but the field has actively pivoted to using QA-accuracy as a reward signal (e.g., CapRL, TimeMaster), directly penalizing models for generating fluent but non-discriminative captions.
*   **Diverging LLM Integration Camps:** Two distinct paradigms dominate TS-LLM alignment without paired captions: "Reprogramming" approaches (like Time-LLM) that map TS patches to the LLM's text embedding space, and "Numeric Tokenization" (like Chronos) that discretizes TS values into an explicit vocabulary for foundation-scale pretraining.
*   **Rise of Agentic and Tool-Augmented TS Systems:** Recent works deploy LLMs as controllers that delegate specific numerical reasoning to external tools, separating the perception/extraction step from the logical deduction step.
*   **Disentangling Perception from Reasoning in Benchmarks:** The community recognized that traditional benchmarks confound domain knowledge with raw shape perception. Emerging suites (like MATHGLANCE for visual/graphical data and Time-MQA) deliberately isolate "shape-aware" visual tasks from multi-step reasoning.
*   **The "Know but Cannot Tell" Captioning Trap:** Vision-language literature provides the empirical negative result confirming your hypothesis: intermediary generated captions consistently underperform direct visual features for downstream QA because models fail to articulate the precise, discriminative features needed for logical deduction, suffering from "template collapse."
